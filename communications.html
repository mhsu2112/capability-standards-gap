Communications: three Spar cycles, with industry rule-making increasingly dominant
Innovations, consortiums, incidents, and regulations plotted against US connected endpoints
Debora Spar's Ruling the Waves argues that new communications technologies pass through four phases: innovation, commercialization, creative anarchy, and rules. The rules phase, she notes, is built from both government regulation and industry-led property rights and standards. Splitting those two kinds of rules into separate lanes (Consortium and Regulation) reveals a shift across the three communications cycles: rule-making moved from state-led (telegraph) to mixed (radio) to predominantly industry-led (internet).
